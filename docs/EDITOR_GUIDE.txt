========================================
   DRIVEOS - GUIDE FOR EDITORS
========================================

A simple guide to using the DriveOS app on your computer.


----------------------------------------
WHAT IS DRIVEOS?
----------------------------------------

DriveOS keeps track of the files on your hard drives, so the
whole team can see in one place:

   - How full each drive is
   - Where the big files are
   - What is safe to clean up

No more digging through folders by hand.


YOUR FILES ARE SAFE:

   - DriveOS only looks at file INFO (names, sizes, dates).
     It NEVER uploads your actual videos or footage.

   - It NEVER deletes anything on its own. When you clean up,
     files are moved to a recoverable "quarantine" first.
     Nothing is ever gone for good.

   - It runs quietly in the background and checks about
     once an hour.


----------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL THE APP
----------------------------------------

ON A MAC:
   1. Open the file "DriveOS Agent.dmg" that you were sent.
   2. Drag the DriveOS Agent icon into your Applications folder.
   3. Open your Applications folder.
   4. RIGHT-CLICK on DriveOS Agent, then click "Open".
   5. Click "Open" again when it asks.

   (You must right-click the very first time. If you just
    double-click, your Mac will block it. This is normal for
    new apps.)

ON WINDOWS:
   1. Run the "DriveOS Agent Setup.exe" installer.
   2. Follow the simple prompts.

After installing, a small DriveOS icon sits in the top-right
menu bar (Mac) or the bottom-right tray (Windows). Click it
anytime to open the app.


----------------------------------------
STEP 2: CONNECT YOUR COMPUTER (one time)
----------------------------------------

   1. Open the DriveOS app. You will see a "Connect" screen.

   2. Ask your admin for a "connect token."
      (They create it on the DriveOS website.)

   3. Type a name for your computer, for example
      "Editing-Bay-1".

   4. Paste the token.

   5. Click "Connect."

That's it. DriveOS is now set up and will scan your drives
automatically every hour.


----------------------------------------
STEP 3: USING THE APP
----------------------------------------

The main screen has a few simple buttons:

   SCAN NOW
      Check your drives right now, instead of waiting for
      the hourly scan.

   ADD DRIVE
      Choose a folder or drive you want DriveOS to keep
      track of.

   PAUSE / RESUME
      Temporarily stop the hourly scans (for example, during
      a big render). Click Resume when you're ready again.

   START AUTOMATICALLY AT LOGIN
      Turn this on so DriveOS opens by itself when you start
      your computer.

The screen also shows:

   - Status ............ Running or Paused
   - Last sync ......... when it last checked your drives
   - Tracked folders ... the drives it is watching
   - Recent activity ... a simple log of what it has done


----------------------------------------
THE WEBSITE DASHBOARD
----------------------------------------

Everything DriveOS finds also shows up on the DriveOS website.
Your admin will give you the link and your login.

There you can see drive space, duplicates, large files, and
cleanup suggestions. It is safe to look around - nothing on
the website can delete your files.

TO LOG IN THE FIRST TIME:
   - Enter your email (the one your admin added for you).
   - Choose any password you like.
   - That becomes your password from then on.


----------------------------------------
COMMON QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------

Q: Will it slow down my computer?
A: No. It scans lightly, once an hour, in the background.
   You can Pause it anytime.

Q: Will it upload my footage?
A: Never. Only file info (names and sizes), never the
   actual videos.

Q: Can it delete my files?
A: No. Cleanup only moves files to a recoverable quarantine.
   You can always restore them.

Q: It says a drive isn't found. Is something broken?
A: No. That just means a drive is unplugged. DriveOS skips
   it safely. Plug it back in and it picks up again.

Q: How do I stop it for a while?
A: Click Pause. Click Resume when you're done.


----------------------------------------
NEED HELP?
----------------------------------------

Ask your DriveOS admin. They can add your account, give you
a connect token, and answer any questions.
